About ANCHORMPCS

A Smart Cooperative Built for Impact

Conceived in 2012 by Engr. Funsho Olaleye and officially registered on 5th May 2024, ANCHORMPCS is more than a savings group — it is a complete growth ecosystem that trains, connects, and empowers members to thrive financially in the 21st-century economy.

Beyond the Loan

We equip members at no cost with digital skills and ICT knowledge for the modern economy.

Community CSR

Our corporate social responsibility targets food security, environmental sustainability, and moral re-armament.

Trusted Governance

Every membership is reviewed by the Management Committee. Your savings are protected and transparently managed.

Our Business Arms

Five Sectors of Growth

ANCHORMPCS operates across five strategic sectors — creating diversified income streams and real opportunities for every member.

Education

Marketing of educational software, entrepreneurship training, and placement of qualified teachers in private schools across Nigeria.

Health

Authorized distributors of premium health products, promoting wellness and generating income across our member communities.

Real Estate

Buying and selling of land and landed properties. Members can target savings to own at least 1 plot of land every year.

Forex & Crypto

Active forex trading, buying and holding digital assets, and free training so every member can earn from global financial markets.

Agriculture

Production, packaging, processing, export, and marketing of agricultural goods — driving food security and cooperative profit.

Growing Portfolio

Our business arms continue to expand. Members share profits and dividends generated across every sector of the cooperative.
